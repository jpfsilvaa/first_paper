\section{Operating Theatre Scheduling\label{sec:ot}}
% ********************************************* %

Following the proposed integrated framework, the final step is a MIP formulation for the Operating Theatre Scheduling Problem (OTSP), which addresses the tactical allocation of subspecialties to operating theatre blocks. 
This model receives as input both the admission volumes determined by the $(R,Q)$ waiting-list control policy and the effective number of surgeries per block derived from the cancellation and overtime management stage. 
By integrating these inputs, the formulation ensures that the resulting schedules are consistent with long-term waiting-list stability while explicitly accounting for the possibility of cancellations at the operational level.

The Objective Functions (OFs) are organised according to the priority levels established by the hospital's guidelines -- although they can be reordered as needed to reflect different institutional priorities in other settings.
To reflect these priorities, we adopt a hierarchical optimisation strategy, as proposed by~\citet{Aringhieri2022Hierarchical}.
The OFs are optimised sequentially, while respecting the optimal values obtained in the previous stages.

Formally, the first OF is defined as
\begin{align}
    \text{Min } \displaystyle F_0 = \sum_{\blockidx \in \blockset} \sum_{\specialtyidx \in \specialtyset} \teamslackvar + \anestconstant \cdot \anestslackvar, \label{eq:f0}
\end{align}
which minimises the total slack in team and anesthesiologist availability, with a high penalty on the latter as they are more scarce and expensive.
The second function is given by
\begin{align}
    \text{Min } \displaystyle F_1 = \sum_{\roomidx \in \roomset} \sum_{\blockidx \in \blockset} \sum_{\specialtyidx \in \specialtyset} \blockvar  \text{ only for variables where } \allowedrooms_{\roomidx\blockidx\specialtyidx} = 0 \label{eq:f1}
\end{align}
which minimises the number of allocations that violate the usual availability of rooms for each subspecialty, thus promoting the use of preferred rooms and reducing the need for last-minute adjustments.
The third objective function is defined as
\begin{align}
    \text{Max } \displaystyle F_2 = \sum_{\roomidx \in \roomset} \sum_{\blockidx \in \blockset} \sum_{\specialtyidx \in \specialtyprset} \blockvar, \label{eq:f2}
\end{align}
which maximises the allocation of priority subspecialties, ensuring that they receive as much capacity as possible within the constraints of the system.
The fourth OF is given by
\begin{align}
\text{Max } \displaystyle F_3 = \sum_{\roomidx \in \roomset} \sum_{\blockidx \in \blockset} \sum_{\specialtyidx \in \specialtyset} \blockvar, \label{eq:f3}
\end{align}
which maximises the total number of allocated blocks, thus promoting overall capacity utilisation and service provision.
Finally, the fifth OF is defined as
\begin{align}
    \text{Max } \displaystyle F_4 = \sum_{\roomidx \in \roomset} \sum_{\blockidx \in \blockset} \sum_{\specialtyidx \in \specialtyset} \blockvar \label{eq:f4}
\end{align}
which maximises the number of sequentially allocated blocks.
This is particularly important for improving the logistics of the surgical process, reducing the need for equipment and team transfers between theatres.

With this, we formulate the theatre planning problem at \hcunicamp with the model \modelname, incorporating these OFs and constraints based on the hospital's current operational framework, but with the flexibility to accommodate other settings as needed.

\begin{alignat}{4}
    (\modelname) \quad & \omit\rlap{Optimize $F_0$--$F_5$}\nonumber\\
    & \mbox{subject to} && \quad & \sum_{\roomidx \in \roomset} \sum_{\blockidx \in \blockset} \blockvar  & \leq \upperq                    &\qquad & \forall\, \specialtyidx \in \specialtyset  \label{eq:c1}\\
    &                  && \quad & \sum_{\roomidx \in \roomset} \sum_{\blockidx \in \blockset} \blockvar  & \geq \lowerq   &\qquad & \forall\, \specialtyidx \in \specialtyset \label{eq:c2}\\
    &                  && \quad & \sum_{\specialtyidx \in \specialtyset} \blockvar                       & \leq 1     &\qquad & \forall\, \roomidx \in \roomset,\, \blockidx \in \blockset \label{eq:c3}\\
    &                  && \quad & \sum_{\roomidx \in \roomset} \sum_{\specialtyidx \in \specialtyset} \needanest_{\specialtyidx} \cdot \blockvar   & \leq \numbersanests_{\blockidx} + \anestslackvar &\qquad & \forall\, \blockidx \in \blockset \label{eq:c4}\\
    &                  && \quad & \sum_{\roomidx \in \roomset} \blockvar   & \leq \specteams_{\blockidx\specialtyidx} + \teamslackvar  &\qquad & \forall\, \,\blockidx \in \blockset,\,\specialtyidx \in \specialtyset \label{eq:c5}\\
    &                  && \quad & \blockvar + x_{\roomidx(\blockidx+1)\specialtyidx} - 1   & \leq \precvar   &\qquad & \forall\, \roomidx \in \roomset,\, \blockidx \in \blockset,\,\specialtyidx \in \specialtyset, \nonumber\\
    &                  &&       &                                                          &                 &\qquad & \planninghorizonidx \in \planninghorizonset :\,\text{ $\blockidx$ and $\blockidx+1$ are blocks} \nonumber\\
    &                  &&       &                                                          &                 &\qquad & \text{ of the same day $\planninghorizonidx$}\label{eq:c6}\\
    &                  && \quad & \blockvar + x_{\roomidx(\blockidx+1)\specialtyidx}       & \geq 2 \cdot \precvar   &\qquad & \forall\, \roomidx \in \roomset,\, \blockidx \in \blockset,\,\specialtyidx \in \specialtyset, \planninghorizonidx \in \planninghorizonset : \nonumber\\
    &                  &&       &                                                          &                 &\qquad & \planninghorizonidx \in \planninghorizonset : \text{ $\blockidx$ and $\blockidx+1$ are blocks} \nonumber\\
    &                  &&       &                                                          &                 &\qquad & \text{ of the same day $\planninghorizonidx$}\label{eq:c7}\\
    &                  && \quad & \blockvar       & = x_{\roomidx(\blockidx+1)\specialtyidx}   &\qquad & \forall\, \roomidx \in \roomset,\, \blockidx \in \blockmset,\,\specialtyidx \in \specialtymset,\, \nonumber\\
    &                  &&       &                                                          &                 &\qquad & \planninghorizonidx \in \planninghorizonset : \text{ $\blockidx$ and $\blockidx+1$ are blocks} \nonumber\\
    &                  &&       &                                                          &                 &\qquad & \text{ of the same day $\planninghorizonidx$}\label{eq:c8}\\
    &                  && \quad & \sum_{\roomidx \in \roomset} \blockvar   & = 2 \cdot \integervar  & \qquad & \forall\, \blockidx \in \blockset,\,\specialtyidx \in \specialtytxset  \label{eq:c9}\\
    &                  &&       & \blockvar                           & \in \{0,1\}                 &        & \forall\, \roomidx \in \roomset,\, \blockidx \in \blockset,\, \specialtyidx \in \specialtyset\nonumber \\
    &                  &&       & \precvar                            & \in \{0,1\}                 &        & \forall\, \roomidx \in \roomset,\, \planninghorizonidx \in \planninghorizonset,\, \specialtyidx \in \specialtyset\nonumber \\
    &                  &&       & \integervar                         & \in \mathbb{Z_{+}}          &        & \forall\, \blockidx \in \blockset,\, \specialtyidx \in \specialtyset\nonumber\\
    &                  &&       & \anestslackvar                      & \in \mathbb{Z_{+}}          &        & \forall \, \blockidx \in \blockset\nonumber\\
    &                  &&       & \teamslackvar                       & \in \mathbb{Z_{+}}.          &        & \forall \, \blockidx \in \blockset,\, \specialtyidx \in \specialtyset \nonumber
\end{alignat}

\bigskip

Constraints~\eqref{eq:c1} and~\eqref{eq:c2} impose minimum and maximum bounds on the number of allocations of each subspecialty.
This is part of the integration with the strategic layer, as these bounds are determined based on the demand $\demand_{\specialtyidx}$ for each subspecialty, which in turn is influenced by the $(R,Q)$ policy.
Constraint~\eqref{eq:c3} ensures that each theatre and time block receives at most one subspeciality.
Constraint~\eqref{eq:c4} is a soft constraint that limits the number of allocations that require anaesthetists according to their availability $\numbersanests_{\blockidx}$ in each block, while allowing for some flexibility through the slack variable $\anestslackvar$.
Constraint~\eqref{eq:c5} similarly limits the number of allocations for each subspecialty based on the availability of medical teams $\specteams_{\blockidx\specialtyidx}$, with the slack variable $\teamslackvar$ providing flexibility when necessary.
Constraints~\eqref{eq:c6} and~\eqref{eq:c7} together guarantee sequential allocation of blocks on the same day when $\precvar = 1$.
Subspecialities requiring two consecutive blocks are specifically addressed by constraint~\eqref{eq:c8}.
Constraint~\eqref{eq:c9} ensures that transplant subspecialties are allocated in pairs, reflecting the need for simultaneous operating theatre use for donor and recipient procedures.tion balancing in conjunction with OF~\eqref{eq:f2}.
